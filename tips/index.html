For the record

Game mischief only.

This tip line is part of the Broomstick Challenge neighborhood game, not an emergency or public-safety reporting service.

Useful intelligence

  • What happened, and approximately when.
  • The team involved, if known.
  • Photos or short clips you have permission to share.
  • Corrections, sightings, and story ideas are welcome.

Keep it neighborly.

Leave out addresses, phone numbers, license plates, and private details. Don't include uninvolved people or material that targets children.

Be clever and fun, but don't be mean.

Evidence checklist

Up to 5 photos or short videos, 10 MB each. Please crop or blur private details before uploading.

A PNN report does not replace posting photo or video proof to the game group for prank points.
