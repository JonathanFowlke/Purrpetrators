SPECIAL REPORT / THE PINK PLANNING DESK

Go big.
Or go haunt.

Every elaborate pink plan begins with a reasonable idea. The difficulty, Inspector Clueso suspects, is getting anyone to leave it reasonable.

The Prowling Purrpetrators remain guilty until proven guilty. This edition examines their planning philosophy, suspicious science, and considerable confidence.

The three stages of Purrpetrator engineering

01 / The reasonable concept
Small. Simple. Entirely achievable. Naturally, this raises objections.

02 / The logistical reality check
Someone explains how much work it will take. The committee mistakes this for a challenge.

03 / The Purrpetrator clause
“What if we actually did it?” The sensible version is quietly removed from the minutes.

Clueso's planning model: a five-minute idea with a three-day production department. Clever and fun remains the brief. Being mean does not improve the design.

THE PINK ADVANTAGE / SPOOF SCIENCE DESK

Peer review pending.
Victory already assumed.

The fictional Institute of Competitive Chromatics has supplied the following figures. These are comedy statistics, not research or game standings. The decimal points are doing most of the work.

+34.7%
Claimed pink win advantage. Apparently, confidence now comes with a spreadsheet.
11.7 seconds
Claimed opponent realization time. Clueso was suspicious considerably sooner.
+13.1%
Claimed October surge. Even the calendar has been recruited into the publicity department.

PNN assessment: a remarkable amount of certainty for a color. Clueso has requested the methodology. Preferably in something other than pink ink.

THE MASCOT FILE / ORIGIN OF THE ATTITUDE

Same cat.
Different level of confidence.

The pink mascot mythology has three essential skills: remain calm, raise one eyebrow, and walk away as though the outcome was arranged last Tuesday. Sunglasses are apparently a strategic qualification.

Turn up late? Call it an entrance. Complicated plan? Adjust the sunglasses. Something goes wrong? Maintain the walk until someone else starts doubting themselves.

Clueso's finding: the Prowling Purrpetrators have confused looking unbothered with having an alibi. Annoyingly, it photographs well.
