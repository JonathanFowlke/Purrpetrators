Game mischief only.

This desk is part of the Broomstick Challenge neighborhood game, not a legal, safety, or complaints service. PNN is fictional, and so are its rulings.

Useful corrections

  • Which case, newsletter, or page, and what it says.
  • What actually happened, and when.
  • Photos or short clips you have permission to share.
  • Your side of the story. Clueso may still disagree.

Keep it neighborly.

Leave out addresses, phone numbers, license plates, and private details. Don't include people who aren't playing, or anything that targets children.

Be clever and fun, but don't be mean.

What happens next?

Private review. A human reads every submission and checks it against the game rules.

Publication only with permission. Nothing goes public automatically. Accurate corrections fix the record.
